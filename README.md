# KRiEC-Tech-SIH-2024

## **🌱 Smart Irrigation and Crop Management System - SIH 2024**

### **🌍 Overview**
We proudly participated in the **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2024** under the **Hardware Edition** category! 🚀 

Our project, **KRiEC-Tech-SIH-2024**, is an innovative **smart irrigation system** designed to optimize water usage in agriculture. By integrating **IoT sensors, real-time weather data, and intelligent decision-making**, our system ensures precise and efficient irrigation, promoting sustainable farming. 🌿💧

### **🔑 Key Features**
- ✅ **Intelligent Irrigation Management** – Determines the exact amount of water required based on real-time environmental factors.
- ✅ **Real-Time Data Monitoring** – Tracks **soil moisture, humidity, and temperature** with IoT sensors.
- ✅ **Dynamic Weather Integration** – Adjusts irrigation schedules using live weather forecasts to prevent water wastage.
- ✅ **Crop & Soil Adaptability** – Customizes irrigation strategies based on different crop types and soil conditions.
- ✅ **Cloud-Based Decision Making** – Uses **Firebase Firestore** to store and analyze data, ensuring smart recommendations.

### **⚙️ How It Works**
Our system collects and processes multiple data points to make irrigation decisions intelligently:
1. **📡 Sensor Data Acquisition** – IoT sensors measure **soil moisture, humidity, and temperature** in real-time.
2. **🌦️ Weather-Based Adjustments** – Retrieves live weather data to dynamically modify irrigation schedules.
3. **📊 Smart Analytics & Recommendations** – Analyzes soil and crop conditions to provide **optimized irrigation strategies**.
4. **☁️ Cloud Integration** – Stores data in **Firebase Firestore**, enabling real-time access and updates.
5. **⚙️ Automated & Manual Control** – Supports **automated irrigation** with a **manual override** option for flexibility.

### **🛠️ Technologies Used**
🔹 **Backend & Database** – Firebase (Firestore Database)  
🔹 **Frontend** – HTML, CSS, JavaScript  
🔹 **IoT Integration** – Sensors for soil moisture, temperature, and humidity  
🔹 **Weather API** – Fetches real-time weather data for intelligent decision-making
